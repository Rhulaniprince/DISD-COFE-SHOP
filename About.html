Our Story

Skinner Café opened its doors in Braamfontein in mid-2025 and quickly became a centrepiece for local runners, creative meetups, and media productions on Juta Street.

We're more than a coffee shop — we're a coworking space and cultural anchor, blending premium coffee with the energy of the inner-city creative scene.

Our Mission

To be Braamfontein's Neighbourhood Creative Hub. A reliable, welcoming space for students, digital creators and artists to work, connect and create. An urban anchor that sparks organic collaboration and good conversation And a place that keeps inner-city food and coffee culture alive, with a rotating menu of artisanal bakes and treats.

What Makes Us Different

Skinner Café isn't just a place to grab a coffee — it's a coworking space and cultural anchor, blending premium coffee with the inner-city creative scene. Whether you're a student between classes, a freelancer needing a change of scenery or just someone who loves good coffee, Skinner Café is your space on Juta Street.

Who We're For

  • University students
  • Young professionals & freelancers
  • Coffee lovers
  • Tourists
  • The local creative community
