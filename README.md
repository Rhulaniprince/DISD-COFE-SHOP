## Project Overview
Skinner Café is a small business (coffee shop & coworking space) located at
70 Juta Street, corner of Juta and De Beer Street, Braamfontein, Johannesburg.
Owned by the Play Braamfontein property group, Skinner Café opened in mid-2025
and has become a hub for local creatives, students, freelancers, and the
inner-city community on Juta Street.

Skinner Café currently has no dedicated website — its only online presence is
via Instagram, TikTok, and the Play Braamfontein venue booking page. This
project delivers a responsive, custom-built website for Skinner Café using
HTML5, CSS, and (in later parts) JavaScript, to improve discoverability,
support local SEO, and give the café a proper digital home.

## Website Goals and Objectives
- Promote the café and its brand
- Display the menu and featured drinks
- Allow customers to enquire about rooftop bookings
- Showcase events and promotions
- Provide location, opening hours, and contact information
- Increase customer engagement

## Key Features and Functionality
- **Home page** — full-width hero banner with tagline, short "our story"
  intro linking to the About page, feature highlights (Creative Hub,
  Rotating Menu, Community First, Prime Location), featured/popular drink
  picks linking to the full menu, and a gallery preview.
- **About page** — café history, mission, what makes Skinner Café different,
  and its target audience.
- **Menu page** — categorised drink and food listings (coffee, iced lattes,
  freezos, tea, soft drinks, breakfast) with real prices, plus café address
  and opening hours.
- **Gallery page** — a responsive grid of photos and video clips showing the
  café space and drinks.
- **Contact page** — enquiry form (name, email, phone, comment) for general
  questions and rooftop booking requests. *(Google Maps embed planned — see
  Changelog.)*
- **Footer** — social links (Instagram, TikTok) and copyright, shared across
  every page.
- **Responsive design** — layout adapts across desktop, tablet, and mobile
  breakpoints throughout the site.

## Part 1 Details
Part 1 covers the site's static front-end structure and styling:
- Semantic HTML5 markup for all core pages (Home, About, Menu, Gallery,
  Contact)
- CSS styling using a warm, natural green colour palette reflecting the
  café's cosy ambiance
- Header, navigation, and footer shared consistently across all pages

Part 2 and Part 3 will follow in future submissions/edits:
- **Part 2** will focus on CSS — refining and completing the site's styling
  and responsive design.
- **Part 3** will focus on JavaScript — adding interactivity, and, per the
  original proposal, working toward a content management system so café
  staff can update the menu and content without coding knowledge.
